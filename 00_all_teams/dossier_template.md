# Deployment dossier – template

DITEC HS26 · one dossier per team · draft due Sun 08.11 (5–8 pages), final version in the final report (Sun 06.12)

**Rules**
- **Deployments, not articles.** Every team answers the same seven questions, so the clusters can be compared at the end.
- **Source tags.** Every number carries its source tag – see [source_tagging_guide.md](source_tagging_guide.md).
- **Not a summary.** Where the sources disagree, say so and explain which one you trust and why.

---

## 0 · Summary (max. ½ page)
The deployment(s) in three sentences, the built-in tension of your cluster, and your answer to it.

## 1 · Context
Venue type, layout (distances, aisles, tables), peak volumes, opening hours, staffing before the robot, why the operator bought it.

## 2 · Robot
Model, dimensions, sensors, navigation approach, payload, speed, battery and docking, interaction features.
**Use spec sheets, not press releases.**

## 3 · Business model
Purchase vs robot-as-a-service (RaaS), price, contract terms, claimed payback, who pays for integration and maintenance.
**Business case at Swiss wages:** use the ballpark figures ([market_figures_and_prices.md](market_figures_and_prices.md), [../staff_shortage_and_wages_dach.md](../staff_shortage_and_wages_dach.md)) or your own figures with a reason.

## 4 · Operational evidence
Deliveries per day, food-to-table time, uptime, interventions per shift, utilisation over time.
**Every figure tagged:** vendor claim / integrator claim / company statement / press / independent study / official statistics / own observation.

## 5 · Staff and customer response
- **Guests:** what they said and did, the novelty effect, acceptance drivers and problems.
- **Staff:** what changed in their work, fears, naming, how they were involved.
- **Required for every team:** who carries the extra work (fixing, recharging, recovering from failures), and who gets the gains?

## 6 · Failure modes
- **Technical:** crowds, chairs, children, elevators, Wi-Fi, hot drinks.
- **Organisational:** management change, ownership of the robot, staff resistance.
- **Economic:** location, no measurable margin, low utilisation.
- Include at least one pause, removal or failure – from your cluster or from the anchor readings.

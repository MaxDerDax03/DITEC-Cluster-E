# Field work: observation and interview guide

DITEC HS26 · for teams collecting primary data (pool S, or any site you can reach)

One lunch peak of observation plus one 20-minute staff interview is worth more than any article on the
reference list – and it is graded that way. Sites near Rotkreuz are in [../pool_s_switzerland_dach](../pool_s_switzerland_dach/README.md).

## Before you go

- **Deployment still running?** Check the website or reviews, or call. If the robot is gone, ask why – that is data.
- **Permission:** ask the manager, explain the course and what you will do with the data.
- **Privacy:**
  - no photos or videos of identifiable guests or staff without consent;
  - no names in your notes;
  - no recording of interviews without explicit consent.
- **Team of two:** one observes, one writes. Agree who counts what.

## Observation sheet (60 minutes during a peak)

| Time | Event | Count / detail |
|---|---|---|
| hh:mm | Robot delivery started (from where to which table) | |
| hh:mm | Delivery completed – who took the items (guest / staff) | |
| hh:mm | Robot stopped or waited because of a person / chair / other | |
| hh:mm | Staff intervention (push, reset, reroute, reload) – how long | |
| hh:mm | Near miss with a guest, child, bag, chair | |
| hh:mm | Guest reaction (photo, talking to the robot, ignoring, complaining) | |
| hh:mm | Robot idle or charging | |

**Summary afterwards:**
- deliveries per hour;
- interventions per hour;
- share of deliveries completed without help;
- where the robot got stuck (sketch the layout);
- occupancy of the room (rough %).

## Staff interview (20 minutes) – server, runner or kitchen staff

1. How long do you work here, and how long has the robot been here?
2. What does the robot do on a normal day – and what does it not do?
3. What changed in your work since the robot came? What got easier, what got harder?
4. Who looks after it when it gets stuck, needs charging or a table plan changes? How often does that happen?
5. How was the team informed and involved before it arrived? Did anyone worry about their job?
6. Does it have a name? Who chose it?
7. How do guests react – first-time visitors vs regulars, children, older guests?
8. If you could change one thing about the robot, what would it be?
9. Would you recommend it to another restaurant? Why or why not?

## Manager or owner interview (20 minutes)

1. Why did you get the robot – which problem should it solve?
2. Purchase or rental? What does it cost per month, and what was not in the offer (installation, training, Wi-Fi, repairs)?
3. How do you measure whether it pays off? What are the numbers so far?
4. How many staff hours does it save – or cost?
5. What happened with staffing since the robot came?
6. What were the biggest problems in the first three months?
7. How did you involve your team? What would you do differently?
8. Would you buy it again today?

## Interview request (e-mail or in person)

**English:** "We are students at Lucerne University of Applied Sciences (HSLU) studying how service robots
work in restaurants. Could we observe your robot during one lunch service and talk to a staff member for
about 20 minutes? We will not take photos of guests or record names, and we are happy to share our results
with you."

**Deutsch:** «Wir sind Studierende der Hochschule Luzern (HSLU) und untersuchen, wie Serviceroboter in der
Gastronomie funktionieren. Dürften wir Ihren Roboter während eines Mittagsservices beobachten und etwa
20 Minuten mit einer Mitarbeiterin oder einem Mitarbeiter sprechen? Wir fotografieren keine Gäste, notieren
keine Namen und stellen Ihnen unsere Ergebnisse gern zur Verfügung.»

## In the dossier

- **Method:** place, date, duration, who observed, what was counted.
- **Results:** tag them **[own]**; quote staff anonymously ("server, 3 years in the restaurant").
- **Limits:** one day, one shift, one observer – say so.

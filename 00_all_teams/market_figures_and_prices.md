## 1 · IFR World Robotics – professional service robots (units sold worldwide)

| Data year | Professional service robots, total | Transportation & logistics | Hospitality | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 158,000 (+48 %) | more than 86,000 (+44 %) | more than 24,500 (+125 %) | [IFR] World Robotics 2023, press release 12.10.2023 – https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/staff-shortage-boosts-service-robots-sales-up-48 |
| 2023 | more than 205,000 (+30 %) | nearly 113,000 (+35 %) | more than 54,000 (+31 %) | [IFR] World Robotics 2024, press release 08.10.2024 – https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/sales-of-service-robots-up-30-worldwide |
| 2024 | almost 200,000 (+9 %); the executive summary says "more than 199,000" | 102,900 (+14 %) | more than 42,000 (−11 %) | [IFR] World Robotics 2025, press release 07.10.2025 – https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/service-robots-see-global-growth-boom · executive summary: https://ifr.org/img/worldrobotics/Executive_Summary_WR_2025_Service_Robots.pdf |

**Further 2024 figures** (World Robotics 2025):
- The robot-as-a-service (RaaS) fleet grew by 31 %, to more than 24,500 units according to the executive summary.
- Professional cleaning: more than 25,000 units (+34 %).
- Medical: about 16,700 units (+91 %).
- Consumer service robots: close to 20 million units (+11 %).

**What "hospitality" means at IFR:** it is **not** the same as restaurant delivery robots. According to the IFR releases, most hospitality units are robots for mobile guidance, information and telepresence; food and beverage preparation is named as an emerging application. Delivery robots such as BellaBot and Servi are split across categories. How exactly they are split is **not verified**.

**Warning: IFR revises previous-year data.** The 2024 growth rates do not match the 2023 figures published a year earlier. [CALC] Back-calculated 2023 values implied by the 2025 edition:
- Total: 199,000 / 1.09 ≈ 182,600 (published in 2024: 205,000)
- Transportation & logistics: 102,900 / 1.14 ≈ 90,300 (published: 113,000)
- Hospitality: 42,000 / 0.89 ≈ 47,200 (published: 54,000)

Always state which edition a figure comes from.

**Next update:** World Robotics 2026 will be published on **24.09.2026** (https://ifr.org/worldrobotics). Replace the 2024 figures with the new edition's figures if you quote them after that date.

### CAGR from IFR figures of the same series [CALC]

Formula: CAGR = (end / start)^(1 / years) − 1. Period: 2022 → 2024, so years = 2. The start value is from the 2023 edition and the end value from the 2025 edition, so the revision warning above applies.

| Series | Calculation | CAGR |
|---|---|---|
| Professional service robots, total | (199,000 / 158,000)^(1/2) − 1 = 1.2595^0.5 − 1 | **≈ 12.2 % p.a.** |
| Transportation & logistics | (102,900 / 86,000)^(1/2) − 1 = 1.1965^0.5 − 1 | **≈ 9.4 % p.a.** |
| Hospitality | (42,000 / 24,500)^(1/2) − 1 = 1.7143^0.5 − 1 | **≈ 30.9 % p.a.** |

The hospitality CAGR hides a peak and a decline (+31 % in 2023, −11 % in 2024). A two-point CAGR says nothing about the path in between.

## 2 · Commercial market forecasts – how far they diverge

All three are **[COM] commercial forecasts** sold by market-research firms. Their methods are not public, and the free pages show only headline numbers.

| Publisher, edition | Market definition | Size stated | Forecast | Stated CAGR (period) | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Virtue Market Research, July 2023 | "Restaurant service robots": cooking robots, waiter robots, home-delivery robots | USD 1.29 bn (2023) | USD 3.86 bn (2030) | **17 %** (2024–2030) | https://virtuemarketresearch.com/report/restaurant-service-robot-market |
| Mordor Intelligence (page undated; accessed 13.09.2026) | "Hospitality robots": delivery (39 % share 2025), security, other; hotels, restaurants and bars | USD 0.61 bn (2025) | USD 2.23 bn (2031) | **24.1 %** (2026–2031) | https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/hospitality-robots-market |
| The Business Research Company, September 2026 edition | "Hospitality robots": delivery, front desk, cleaning, other | USD 0.7 bn (2025), 0.9 bn (2026) | USD 2.13 bn (2030) | **24 %** (2026–2030) | https://www.thebusinessresearchcompany.com/report/hospitality-robots-global-market-report |

**How much they diverge:**
- A restaurant-only market (USD 1.29 bn in 2023) is reported as **larger** than two different estimates of the whole hospitality robot market in 2025 (USD 0.61 bn and 0.7 bn).
- The stated CAGRs range from 17 % to 24 %.
- The cause is mainly how each report defines the market (whether it includes cooking and home-delivery robots) rather than different views of reality.

If you quote any of these figures, name the definition next to it.

## 4 · How to use these numbers

- Every figure here is a **starting point**, not the answer. Label each number in your dossier with its source type and date, as in this file.
- You may use other figures, for example a quote from Sebotics, a price from your own interview, or newer IFR data after 24.09.2026, **if you justify them**: say where the figure comes from, what it includes, and why it fits the HSLU cafeteria better.
- Prefer ranges over single values. Run your payback calculation with the low and the high price, and with the wage figures from the DACH briefing.
- Never mix a commercial forecast CAGR with IFR unit figures in one calculation. They measure different things (revenue under one firm's definition vs units counted by IFR).

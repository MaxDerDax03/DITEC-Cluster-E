# Case studies – source packs and material

DITEC HS26 · case-study track

Every team owns **one cluster** of real deployments and writes a deployment dossier about it. The dossier
ends in 3–5 verifiable requirements for your own robot. Two **common pools** are open to every team.
Everything here is links, summaries and templates. No copyrighted articles are stored; open the links yourself.

# Common pool S · Switzerland & DACH field work

Source pack for all teams · links checked 13.09.2026 · DITEC HS26

## Why this pool matters

These robots are within reach of Rotkreuz. Most coverage dates from 2021–2023 and started with the integrator's press releases. Whether they still run in 2026 is largely **unknown** – that gap is your opportunity. One confirmed answer from a call or visit ("still running" or "removed, because …") is primary data. The first Swiss showcase, Andulino, no longer exists.

Wages and staff acceptance: see `../staff_shortage_and_wages_dach.md` and `../staff_acceptance_of_robots.md`.

## Starter questions for your dossier

1. **Context:** Is walking distance (mall hall, museum, lodge) the real driver?
2. **Robot:** Which speed settings do operators use, and who mapped the site?
3. **Business model:** CHF 21,900 vs trial rental – payback at about CHF 22–25/h?
4. **Operational evidence:** Deliveries and interventions counted in one lunch peak?
5. **Staff and customer response:** What do staff say in 2026 that 2021 press releases did not?
6. **Failure modes:** Business closure (Andulino) vs "put in a corner" at peak (Verkehrshaus)?
7. **Transfer to HSLU:** Which observed failure becomes a TurtleBot 4 test scenario?

## Field work – how to approach a site

**Before you go**
- Ask permission first: use the public contact form, or ask the manager in a quiet hour. Say what you will record and how long you will stay.
- Confirm the robot is still in use. "Removed, because …" is a result too.
- Agree on a place to stand that does not block staff routes.

**Observation during a lunch peak (e.g. 11:30–13:30)**
- Work in pairs: one person counts, one keeps a time-stamped log.
- Count per 15-minute block:
  - robot deliveries and clearing trips, and human trips on the same routes;
  - interventions: someone pushes, restarts, re-sends or clears the path for the robot;
  - stops or detours longer than about 10 s;
  - near misses: a guest, child or staff member has to step aside or stop;
  - guests who refuse the robot.
- Note aisle widths (estimate), pulled-out chairs, and when the robot is parked.
- Tag every number *own observation*. Use A5 (Çalışkan & Sevim) as the template.

**Privacy and respect**
- No photos or video of identifiable guests or staff without consent. Photograph the robot and empty areas only.
- Do not record conversations. Take notes without names, and nothing that rates an individual staff member.
- If staff or management ask you to stop, stop.

**20-minute staff interview – request text**

> *English:* We are students of the BSc International IT Management at Lucerne University of Applied Sciences and Arts (HSLU). In a course project we study how service robots such as the BellaBot work in everyday restaurant operation. Could we talk to you or a member of your team for about 20 minutes, at a time that suits you outside the lunch peak? We will not use names, and we will be happy to share our findings with you.

> *Deutsch:* Wir sind Studierende des Bachelors International IT Management an der Hochschule Luzern (HSLU). In einem Kursprojekt untersuchen wir, wie sich Serviceroboter wie der BellaBot im Restaurantalltag bewähren. Dürften wir mit Ihnen oder jemandem aus Ihrem Team etwa 20 Minuten sprechen – zu einer Zeit ausserhalb des Mittagsbetriebs, die Ihnen passt? Wir verwenden keine Namen und teilen unsere Ergebnisse gerne mit Ihnen.

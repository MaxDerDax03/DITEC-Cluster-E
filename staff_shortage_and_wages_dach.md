# Staff shortage or pay problem? Hospitality wages in Switzerland, Germany and Austria

Briefing for the case-study track · status 13.09.2026 · check the links before you quote a figure

**Why this matters.** Operators name "we cannot find staff" as the first reason to buy a service robot.
Workers who leave hospitality name pay first. A dossier that repeats the operator's view without the
wage data is one-sided – and the wage level is the biggest variable in the robot business case.

Source types: **[OFF]** official statistics · **[CBA]** collective agreement · **[ASSOC]** industry
association or union · **[MEDIA]** media report

## Switzerland

- **[CBA] L-GAV minimum wages 2026** (gross per month, 13 salaries after probation): Ia unskilled CHF 3,713 · Ib (Progresso) 3,943 · II (EBA) 4,070 · IIIa (EFZ) 4,528 · IIIb 4,635 · IV 5,293. The 2026 increase compensated inflation only. Ia/Ib can be up to 8 % lower during induction.
  L-GAV social partners, 20.10.2025 – https://l-gav.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/20251020-mi-mindestl%C3%B6hne-2026-l-gav-gastgewerbe-DE.pdf · GastroSuisse leaflet 2026 – https://gastrosuisse.ch/assets/de/recht/l-gav/gastgewerbliche-loehne/gastrosuisse-merkblatt-gastgewerbliche-loehne-2026.pdf
- **Hourly rate – own calculation, not an official figure:** CHF 3,713 at 42 h/week (L-GAV Art. 15) ≈ CHF 20.40/h; with the 13th salary ≈ CHF 22.10/h; hourly staff with holiday pay ≈ CHF 24–25/h. Lucerne has no cantonal minimum wage.
- **[OFF] BFS wage structure survey 2024** (published 25.11.2025): national median CHF 7,024/month; restaurants CHF 4,744 (68 %), accommodation 4,715, retail 5,214. Low-wage threshold CHF 4,683.
  https://www.admin.ch/de/newnsb/CzozNgBTCJOrNyvwsItyp
- **[OFF] LUSTAT, canton of Lucerne 2024** (published 30.04.2026): hospitality median CHF 4,900 – the lowest of all sectors (all: CHF 6,800); 33 % of hospitality jobs below the low-wage threshold, the highest share.
  https://www.lustat.ch/analysen/wirtschaft-arbeit/2024/loehne
- **[OFF/MEDIA] Vacancies Q2 2026:** all sectors +2.7 % (98,700 open positions); hospitality −21.1 % year on year (the sector figure is from a press report of the BFS release).
  https://www.vtg.admin.ch/de/newnsb/3iJDgJ638lidRZ0ahW4Vp · https://www.swiss-press.com/schweizer-arbeitsmarkt-98700-offene-stellen/1829042

## Germany

- **[OFF] Statutory minimum wage:** €13.90/h from 01.01.2026, €14.60 from 2027 – about €2,400/month full time. Regional collective wages sit slightly above (e.g. NRW lowest band €0.50 above the minimum; 2026 rates not verified). https://www.dehoga-nrw.de/branchenthemen/tarifeundloehne
- **[OFF] Destatis, April 2025 (full time):** hospitality €3,185/month or €18.77/h; all sectors €4,784 or €28.19/h – hospitality is the lowest sector.
  https://www.destatis.de/DE/Themen/Arbeit/Verdienste/Verdienste-Branche-Berufe/Tabellen/bruttomonats-verdienste-stichmonat.html
- **[OFF] Destatis low-wage jobs:** 51 % of hospitality jobs pay below €14.32/h – the highest share of any sector (all sectors: 16 %). Press release 05.12.2025. https://www.destatis.de/DE/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2025/12/PD25_434_623.html
- **[ASSOC with BA data] Shortage has eased:** 17,667 registered hospitality vacancies in January 2026 (−18.3 % year on year, lowest since COVID) against 64,306 unemployed in restaurant and 32,958 in hotel occupations. Labour costs +39.6 % since Q4 2019; real turnover −14.8 % vs 2019 – part of the easing is falling demand.
  DEHOGA Zahlenspiegel IV/2025 – https://www.dehoga-bundesverband.de/fileadmin/Startseite/04_Zahlen___Fakten/DEHOGA-Zahlenspiegel_4._Quartal_2025.pdf
- **[MEDIA] Skilled-worker gap** (IW/KOFA via Handelsblatt, 22.01.2026): about 44,000 in 2023, about 2,700 in July 2024–June 2025.
  https://www.handelsblatt.com/unternehmen/handel-konsumgueter/hotels-und-gaststaetten-fachkraeftemangel-im-gastgewerbe-war-einmal/100191767.html

## Austria

- **[CBA] Kollektivvertrag Hotel- und Gastgewerbe:** tables of 01.05.2025 still apply. Vienna restaurants, wage group 5 (service assistant, kitchen help) €2,026/month; group 3 (trained restaurant specialist, cook) €2,165; plus holiday and Christmas bonus. From 01.10.2026 +3.02 % on average (+3.48 % for group 5).
  https://www.wko.at/kollektivvertrag/lohnordnung-gastronomie-wien-arbeiter-2025 · https://www.wko.at/oe/oesterreich/kv-abschluss-gastronomie-hotellerie
- **[OFF] Income report 2024** (Rechnungshof/Statistik Austria, 2023 data): accommodation and restaurants have by far the lowest median annual income, €15,166 vs €35,314 for all employees – seasonal and part-time work lower the figure; tips are not recorded.
  https://www.rechnungshof.gv.at/rh/home/home_1/home_1/Allgemeiner_Einkommensbericht_2024_BF.pdf
- **[OFF] AMS tourism report 2025:** vacancies peaked at about 21,000 in June 2022; by April 2025 −53 % in restaurants and −69 % in accommodation; tourism unemployment up to 18.2 % (overall 7.2 %).
  https://www.ams.at/content/dam/download/arbeitsmarktdaten/%C3%B6sterreich/berichte-auswertungen/001_spezialthema_0625.pdf

## Why people leave hospitality

- **[ASSOC] NGG union survey, Germany 2022** (about 4,000 respondents, mostly union members): 34 % see no future in the sector; **low pay is named first "by a clear margin"**, then lack of appreciation, psychological strain and working hours that do not fit family life. (A figure of "80 % cite pay" circulates in secondary reports but is not in the NGG document – do not use it.)
  https://www.ngg.net/fileadmin/Hauptverwaltung/Materialien/PDF/20221018__Auswertung-Beschaeftigten-Umfrage-Gastgewerbe.pdf
- **[ASSOC] Hotel & Gastro Union / Ipsos, Switzerland 2021** (2,076 respondents): 18 % intend to leave (2019: 10 %); the biggest barriers are pay and working hours.
  https://www.hotelgastrounion.ch/fileadmin/files/PDF_Dateien/Medienmitteilung_HGU-Umfrage.pdf
- **[Research] FORBA for AK Wien 2023:** evening, night and weekend work mostly without surcharges, split shifts, hours that are hard to plan.
  https://wien.arbeiterkammer.at/service/presse/Forba_Bericht_Hotel_Gastgewerbe_2023.pdf
- **[OFF] IAB short report 6/2023:** in 2020 leavers mainly moved into retail and transport/logistics; returners more often got higher pay than in 2019 (27 % vs 20 %).
  https://doku.iab.de/kurzber/2023/kb2023-06.pdf
- **[Consultancy for HotellerieSuisse] BASS 2022:** operators report conceding higher wages and better schedules (no split shifts, closed weekends) – anecdotal, no measured effect on hiring.
  https://www.buerobass.ch/fileadmin/Files/2022/Hotelleriesuisse_2022_FachkraeftemangelGastgewerbe.pdf

## Use in your dossier

- Put the operator's claim ("no staff") and the wage data next to each other – that is the provenance and triangulation the rubric rewards.
- Use the wage level of your deployment's country in the business case: at Swiss wages the robot's payback looks very different from the US numbers in vendor material.
- Ask in your interviews: what was tried before the robot – higher pay, other shifts, fewer opening hours?

## Caveats

- The Swiss hospitality vacancy change (−21.1 %) comes from a press report of the BFS release.
- The Swiss hourly rates are our own calculation.
- The Austrian 43 % includes part-time and seasonal employment.
- Tips are not included in any official figure.
- The surveys on reasons for leaving are from 2021–2022, and the IAB/IW mobility data from 2020.
- German regional collective wages for 2026 and the BA Entgeltatlas median for waiters could not be verified.
